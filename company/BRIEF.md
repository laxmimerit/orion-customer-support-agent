# Orion company brief

Orion is a fictional AI customer support desk for small software companies. It organises incoming tickets, finds related cases and drafts replies for a person to review. It does not send replies automatically.

Maya owns product and launch decisions. Arun owns engineering. Leena owns customer onboarding. The private beta has a five-company goal, ten working days and a proposed USD 500 spending cap. No pilot companies are confirmed and no product performance claims have been measured.

## Simulation boundary

The January-June support history is an invented operating scenario for analytics and development. The 300 customer companies and nine support agents describe this simulation, not customers already won by the pre-launch startup. July is the beta-planning scenario. Never present synthetic revenue, satisfaction or model outputs as Orion business results.

## Product boundaries

A human reviews generated replies. Classification suggests category and urgency; low-confidence or invalid outputs go to review. Similar-ticket retrieval supports decisions; it does not prove a response is correct. Illustrative plan prices are Starter USD 19, Growth USD 49 and Scale USD 99 per seat/month; Growth changes from USD 39 to USD 49 on 1 April in the historical simulation.

## Audience and needs

Support leads need a manageable queue and reliable handoffs. Founders need clear costs and practical setup. CX directors need trends and service quality. IT managers need controlled access, export and predictable integrations.

## Dataset contract

All records are fictional. UTC timestamps cover January-June, with a fixed snapshot at 30 June 23:59:59 UTC. Historical tickets, held-out evaluation tickets and untriaged backlog have separate IDs and files. Evaluation labels are separate from inputs and excluded from search exports. The fixed generation seed is 20261030.
